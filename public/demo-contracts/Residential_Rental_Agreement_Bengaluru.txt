RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made and executed on this 15th day of October 2026 at Bengaluru, Karnataka by and between:

Mr. R. Raghavan, S/o Late K. Ramaswamy, aged about 58 years, residing at No. 42, 12th Main Road, Indiranagar, Bengaluru - 560038 (hereinafter called the "LESSOR / LANDLORD", which expression shall include his heirs, executors, and legal representatives) of the ONE PART;

AND

Mr. Srikar Joshi, S/o V. Joshi, aged about 29 years, currently working at TechCorp Solutions, residing at Bengaluru (hereinafter called the "LESSEE / TENANT", which expression shall include his legal representatives and assigns) of the OTHER PART.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute lawful owner of Residential Flat No. 302, 3rd Floor, Greenview Heights, 100 Feet Road, Indiranagar, Bengaluru - 560038, consisting of 2 Bedrooms, Living Room, Kitchen, and 2 Bathrooms (hereinafter referred to as the "SCHEDULE PREMISES").

AND WHEREAS the Lessee has requested the Lessor to let out the Schedule Premises for residential tenancy purpose only, and the Lessor has agreed to the same on the following terms and conditions:

1. TENANCY PERIOD & POSSESSION
The tenancy shall be for an initial period of eleven (11) months commencing from 1st November 2026 and expiring on 30th September 2027.

2. MONTHLY RENT & UTILITY CHARGES
(a) The Lessee agrees to pay a monthly rent of INR 42,000/- (Rupees Forty-Two Thousand only) payable in advance on or before the 5th day of each calendar month via NEFT/UPI bank transfer.
(b) In case of delay in payment of rent beyond the 5th day of the month, the Lessee shall pay a late payment fee of INR 250/- per day of delay until full clearance.
(c) Electricity and water consumption charges as per actual meter readings shall be paid directly by the Lessee. Society maintenance of INR 4,500/- per month shall be paid to the Resident Welfare Association.

3. RENT ESCALATION ON RENEWAL
In the event both parties mutually agree to renew this Agreement after the expiry of 11 months, the monthly rent shall automatically increase by ten percent (10%) over the prevailing monthly rent.

4. INTEREST-FREE SECURITY DEPOSIT & PAINTING DEDUCTIONS
(a) The Lessee has paid an interest-free refundable Security Deposit of INR 2,50,000/- (Rupees Two Lakhs Fifty Thousand only) to the Lessor upon execution of this Agreement.
(b) Upon vacation and handover of the Schedule Premises by the Lessee for any reason whatsoever, the Lessor shall deduct one full month rent (INR 42,000/-) from the Security Deposit towards mandatory repainting and deep cleaning charges, irrespective of the duration of the Lessee's stay or the physical condition of the walls.
(c) The balance Security Deposit shall be refunded to the Lessee within thirty (30) days from the date of physical key handover, after deducting any unpaid electricity bills or documented tenant damages.

5. LOCK-IN PERIOD & FORFEITURE OF DEPOSIT
(a) Both parties explicitly agree to a mandatory lock-in period of six (6) months from the commencement date.
(b) If the Lessee vacates or terminates the tenancy prior to the expiry of the 6-month lock-in period, the entire Security Deposit of INR 2,50,000/- shall be unconditionally forfeited to the Lessor as liquidated damages.
(c) After the lock-in period, either party may terminate this agreement by providing sixty (60) days prior written notice.

6. USE OF PREMISES & RESTRICTIONS
(a) The premises shall be used strictly for private residential accommodation and not for any commercial, office, or subletting purposes.
(b) The Lessee shall not make any structural alterations, nail fixtures into granite, or modify electrical wiring without written permission.

7. INSPECTION BY LESSOR
The Lessor or his authorized agent shall have the right to enter and inspect the Schedule Premises at any time between 8:00 AM and 9:00 PM upon giving two (2) hours prior verbal notice via telephone or messaging to the Lessee.

8. GOVERNING LAW & JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India. Any disputes arising out of or in connection with this tenancy shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the civil courts at Bengaluru, Karnataka.
